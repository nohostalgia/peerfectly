## A board started with nobody around stays reachable

A board that could not reach the full member it keeps in touch with called it again after a rest.
While that call waited, the rest it had just served still counted as its next wake-up, already due,
so the board's loop ran again at once, over and over, for the fifteen to twenty seconds of each
call. Nothing else on the board ran in that time: not the link to the relay, not the tunnel, not
the sketch's answers. A board powered on while the computer was off could not be reached from a
phone, and came back the moment the computer did.

- **A rest that is over no longer wakes the board.** The board calls again at the same pace as
  before (after 10, 20 and 40 seconds, then once a minute), and between calls it waits.
- **The board's loop gives way to its other tasks at every round,** so a mistake of this kind can
  no longer starve them.

## A phone or computer that comes up alone is let in by boards

A board holds no roster: it believes a phone or a computer by the proof of membership shown when a
session opens. A phone or computer started showing that proof only after its roster next changed,
which happens when it syncs with another full member. One brought up with no other full member
around showed nothing, and every board refused it until another full member came up.

- **A device shows its proof from the moment it comes up,** and again at once on the new link when
  the relay moves.

## The Arduino examples no longer stall with the IDE closed

On a board whose serial port is its own USB (USB CDC On Boot, as on the ESP32-C3 and S3), a line
printed while the cable is plugged in and nothing reads the port waited up to two seconds, and the
sketch with it. The examples now drop such lines at once (`Serial.setTxTimeoutMs(0)`) after the
board is on its network. A sketch of your own that prints in its loop should do the same.

Nothing changes between devices or with the relay: 0.5.3, 0.5.2, 0.5.1 and 0.5.0 devices work
together. Each fix takes effect where it is installed: rebuild the sketch with this version's
Arduino library for the first, and update phones and computers for the second.
