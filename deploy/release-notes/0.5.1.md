## A device that comes back is reached at once

0.5.0 made a device wait after it failed to reach another: thirty seconds, then twice as long each
time, up to ten minutes, during which nothing was even tried. A phone that tried a board while the
board was restarting, or off its WiFi for a moment, could not reach it for minutes after it was
back. In this version two devices that want to talk do so as soon as both are there:

- **A packet for a device with no session tries to reach it at once,** whatever happened to the last
  attempt. There is still one attempt at a time per device, and an application talking to a device
  that is switched off meets its own timeout, as on any network.
- **What a device does on its own is retried within a minute, never hours:**
  - a neighbour that did not answer is tried again within a minute (was up to six hours);
  - a device you approved while it was off is given the network within a minute of coming back
    (was up to half an hour);
  - a device admitted a moment ago, refused by a member that has not heard of it yet, retries every
    second;
  - a board calls the computer it keeps in touch with within a minute of its return (was up to six
    hours).
- **A device that changes network tries every neighbour again at once,** such as a laptop waking up
  or moving to another WiFi.
- **A board just admitted is reachable within seconds, every time.** About one admission in
  seven or ten left the board without its IPv4 address for a minute or two: the admin and the board
  dialled each other at once, and the board asked for its address over a session the admin had
  dropped. Both ends now agree on which session is open, and the board asks again until it has
  its address.
- **A device whose network was turned off and on again reaches the others at once.** Until one of
  them got in touch first, it could not reach a device it had spoken to before.
- **A board notices when its own network changes,** such as WiFi back after a drop, and reconnects to
  the relay at once rather than a minute or two later. Rebuild the sketch with this version's
  Arduino library.

Nothing changes on the wire: 0.5.1 and 0.5.0 devices work together, and the relay does not change.
Update every device to get the whole of it.
